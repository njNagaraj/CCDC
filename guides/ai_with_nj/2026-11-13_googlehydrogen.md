# Google Search's new AI: 20 questions to copy (@ai_with_nj)

On 12 Nov 2026 Google upgraded Search with its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, "available globally in Google Search for all users" (blog.google). Google says Search now understands nuanced, multi-part questions (typos included), links to a wider range of sites, and gives more visual answers with images, structured responses and comparison tables. AI Mode works in India in English, Hindi and more Indian languages.

## How to open it

- Phone: open the Google app, tap AI Mode in the row with All, Images, News.
- Browser: go to google.com/aimode.

## The 3 tricks

1. Put your whole situation in one question (who, where, budget, what you tried).
2. Ask for a comparison table.
3. List your must-haves. Typos are fine.

## 20 questions to copy (change the details to yours)

Shopping
1. "Best phone under ₹20,000 for camera and battery, I don't play games. Compare 3 in a table."
2. "Slip-on shoes for standing all day, good arch support, breathable, under ₹3,000."
3. "Mixer grinder for a family of 4, makes idli batter, under ₹4,000, quiet. Table please."
4. "Gift ideas for my dad who loves gardening, under ₹1,500, deliverable in Chennai by Friday."
5. "Compare 3 budget laptops for a college student who codes, under ₹50,000."

Travel
6. "3-day Goa trip in December for 2 people under ₹25,000, no clubbing, good food. Day-by-day plan."
7. "Train vs flight vs bus from Chennai to Bengaluru on a weekday: time, cost, comfort. Table."
8. "Quiet hill station within 6 hours of Delhi for a long weekend with my parents."
9. "Things to pack for Ladakh in May for someone who gets altitude sickness."

Home
10. "I work from home in a tiny 1BHK and my bed shows on video calls. Make it feel like 2 rooms under ₹5,000."
11. "My ceiling fan makes a ticking noise at low speed, what should I check before calling an electrician?"
12. "Weekly vegetarian meal plan for 2 people, high protein, under ₹2,000 groceries."
13. "Indoor plants that survive a north-facing balcony in Mumbai with little sun."

Jobs and money
14. "Compare 3 areas near Whitefield, Bengaluru for a 28-year-old: rent, commute, food. Make a table."
15. "Skills to learn in 3 months to move from customer support to data analyst, free resources only."
16. "Explain old vs new tax regime for a salary of ₹12 lakh in simple words with a table."
17. "Questions to ask HR before accepting a job offer at a startup."

Students
18. "Explain photosynthesis to a class 8 student with a simple example and 5 quiz questions."
19. "Compare 3 free online courses for learning Python from zero, with time needed for each."

Hindi
20. "मेरे पास ₹30,000 का बजट है, ऑफिस और पढ़ाई के लिए सबसे अच्छा लैपटॉप कौन सा है? टेबल में तुलना करें।"

## Before you act

- Check prices and stock on the shop's own site.
- Open the linked sources.
- Never type Aadhaar, bank, card or health numbers into a search.
- Gemini app users on the free plan also get Gemini 4 Hydrogen automatically when Google picks it as the right model for a prompt.
