# Google Flow: 5 free artist-made tools (@ai_with_nj)

On 12 Nov 2026 Google added 5 new tools to Flow, its AI creative studio, made with visual artists (blog.google, "5 new Google Flow Tools designed by visual artists"). Free accounts get 50 Flow credits a day, need to be 18+, and India is a supported country (Google Flow Help).

## Direct links to all 5 tools

1. Pixel Machine (pixel art, halftone, glitch, thermal and more): https://flow.google.com/shared/tool/69ba0632-6a20-4e49-b45d-2ff114adf01f
2. Lucid Glass (prisms, halos, refractions, optical blooms): https://flow.google.com/shared/tool/9101b8f7-6c9f-4a14-8835-7e60810e79d3
3. Pocket Studio (new camera angles from one phone video): https://flow.google.com/shared/tool/c951215d-53fb-49dd-9be9-20a93fb955d7
4. Character Poster: https://flow.google.com/shared/tool/57af5526-b061-4c9c-815c-8c410c848bc5
5. Character Arc: https://flow.google.com/shared/tool/60216976-6410-4921-adf8-ec7765d47d09

Flow home: https://flow.google

## Pixel Machine in 4 steps

1. Open the Pixel Machine link above and sign in with your Google account.
2. Upload a photo or short video (or pick one from your Flow library).
3. Pick an effect: Halftone, Embroidery, Pixel Art, Signal Glitch, Tracking, Risograph, Cyanotype, Slit-scan, Solarization or Thermal.
4. Pick a preset (Minimal, Classic, Ultra), use Split to compare with the original, then Download Result.

## Best effect for each use

- Profile picture: Pixel Art (Classic preset)
- Reel cover: Signal Glitch or Tracking
- Shop poster or story background: Risograph or Halftone
- Phone wallpaper: Cyanotype or Thermal
- Product video: run it through Pocket Studio first for extra angles

## Make your own Flow tool

Google's post says you can build your own tools in Flow too. Open Flow, go to Tools, and start a new one from there.

## Know before you start

- Each tool use can consume credits. You get 50 free a day; they don't roll over.
- Use your own photos. Ask before editing someone else's.
- Exact credit cost per tool is not published; check your balance in Flow.
