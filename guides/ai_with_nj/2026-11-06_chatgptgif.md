# ChatGPT GIFs: 10 ready prompts and setup (@ai_with_nj)

On 4 Nov 2026 OpenAI launched ChatGPT Images 2.6. In OpenAI's words: "turn a prompt or image into an animated GIF. Adjust the speed, edit individual frames, and share it on repeat." You can also save reference images of yourself, your pets or characters and reuse them without uploading again.

Sources: OpenAI on X (x.com/OpenAI, 4 Nov 2026) and openai.com/index/introducing-images-2-6 (available in ChatGPT across subscription tiers on desktop, mobile and web). OpenAI has not published how many GIFs each plan allows.

## How to make your first GIF

1. Open the ChatGPT app or chatgpt.com and update the app if needed.
2. Tap + and add a photo, or just describe the scene.
3. Ask for a GIF, for example: "Make this an animated GIF."
4. Change the speed or fix a single frame if something looks off.
5. Download or share it.

## Save a reference image (pets, family, you)

When you add a photo you want to reuse, save it as a reference and give it a name (for example your dog's name). Next time, mention that name in your prompt and ChatGPT uses the saved image. Only save people who agreed to it.

## 10 prompts to copy

Add your photo first, then paste one:

1. Diwali greeting: "Turn this family photo into a looping GIF: diyas flicker around us, soft sparkles fall, warm golden light. Add the text Happy Diwali."
2. Rangoli: "Animate this rangoli photo as a GIF: colours fill in one by one, then a gentle glow."
3. Pet reaction: "Make a short GIF of my dog tilting its head and blinking, loop it."
4. Birthday wish: "Make a GIF from this photo: confetti falls, balloons float up, text Happy Birthday [name]."
5. Thank-you note: "Animate this photo with a slow zoom and the words Thank you appearing letter by letter."
6. Product loop: "Make a GIF of this product slowly turning on a clean white background, seamless loop."
7. Food shot: "Animate this chai photo: steam rises from the cup, loop it."
8. Weekend plan: "Create a GIF of a small car driving along a hill road at sunset, cartoon style, loop."
9. Office reaction: "Create a GIF of a coffee cup giving a thumbs up, simple cartoon, 6 frames."
10. Instagram Story sticker: "Make a small looping GIF of a sparkling New badge on a transparent-looking plain background."

Tip: if the motion is too fast, ask "slow it down" or set the speed in the GIF controls.

## Good manners and safety

- Ask before animating someone else's photo.
- Don't make GIFs that could fool people into thinking something real happened.
- Saved references stay in your account: save only what you are happy to keep there.
