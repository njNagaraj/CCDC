# Hex: free offline voice typing (setup guide, @ai_with_nj)

Hex is a free, open-source (MIT) voice dictation app for Windows, Mac and Linux. Hold a shortcut, speak, let go, and Hex types the text into whatever app you are in. It transcribes on your own computer: the first run downloads a speech model, and after that nothing leaves your machine.

Official repo and downloads: https://github.com/anomalyco/hex (read 3 Nov 2026, Hex 3.3.0). Windows guide: docs/windows.md in the repo.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or ARM64). x64 PCs need a 2013 or newer processor with AVX2 (Intel Haswell / AMD Excavator or later). Windows support is beta.
- Or a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer).
- Or Linux x86_64 / ARM64 (beta).
- A microphone and 1 to 3 GB of free space for the speech model.

## Step 1: download and install

- Windows x64: https://anoma.ly/hex/download/windows-x64-setup
- Windows ARM64: https://anoma.ly/hex/download/windows-arm64-setup
- Mac (Apple silicon): https://anoma.ly/hex/download/macos-arm64-dmg, or `brew install --cask anomalyco/tap/hex`
- Linux: `curl -fsSL https://anoma.ly/hex/install-linux.sh | sh` (or the .deb / .rpm on the README)

Download only from these official links.

## Step 2: first run

Open Hex. The guided first run asks for microphone (and on Mac, accessibility) permission and downloads a speech model. You can also skip and pick a model later (Hex 3.3).

## Step 3: pick the right model

In Settings, Local transcription, choose your language first, then a model.

- English only: Parakeet Unified English (recommended in Hex, 731 MB).
- Hindi: choose Hindi in the language list. Hex offers models that support it, such as Qwen3-ASR and Nemotron 3.5. Whisper large-v3-turbo (886 MB) covers 100 languages.
- Mixed Hindi and English: try Auto, then compare with Hindi selected.

From a terminal you can also run `hex model list` to see every model, its languages and size.

## Step 4: your shortcut

- Windows default: hold Ctrl+Space, speak, let go.
- Mac default: hold Option, speak, let go.
- Change it in Settings, General, Dictation, Shortcut. On Windows a mouse button (Mouse 4 or 5) works too.
- Ctrl+Space clashes with some keyboard layout switchers on Windows. If it does, rebind it.

## Daily tips

- Speak in full sentences and pause before letting go.
- Add names and work words in Vocabulary so Hex spells them right.
- History keeps your dictations so you can copy one again.

## Troubleshooting

- Hex quits at start on Windows: your processor lacks AVX2. Hex does not run on it.
- Nothing is typed: check the microphone permission and that the model finished downloading (Settings shows progress).
- Model download fails: often a proxy or certificate issue. Retry, or use "Add model file" with a model you already have.
- Text goes to the wrong place: click into the text box first, then hold the shortcut.
