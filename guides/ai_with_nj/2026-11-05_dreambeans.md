# Google Dreambeans: safe setup guide (@ai_with_nj)

Dreambeans is an experimental Google Labs app. Overnight it reads the Google apps you choose to connect and gives you a daily set of personalized, illustrated stories: places to visit, topics to explore, things to try and events coming up. Some stories have one-tap actions such as "See Ticket" or "Watch Trailer".

Official page: https://labs.google/dreambeans (read 5 Nov 2026). It is available to users aged 18+ in the US and India, on Android and iPhone. Google launched it in India on 2 Nov 2026 with 13 Indian languages (TechCrunch, quoting Google).

## Step 1: install from the official store

- Android: Google Play, "Dreambeans" by Google LLC (package com.google.android.apps.labs.lotus)
- iPhone: App Store, "Dreambeans" by Google
- Web: labs.google/dreambeans

Check the developer name is Google before you install. Look-alike apps are common after a launch.

## Step 2: connect apps, one at a time

Dreambeans can use Gemini, Gmail, Calendar, Google Photos, YouTube and Search. You choose which ones.

A careful order:

1. Week 1: connect YouTube and Search only. See if the stories are useful.
2. Week 2: add Calendar if you want event reminders as stories.
3. Only then decide on Gmail and Photos. Photos lets stories include pictures of you and your family.

You can change connected apps any time from your profile icon in the app.

## Step 3: pick your language

Google says stories work in 13 Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Punjabi, plus English. Each story uses one language; Hindi and English can't be mixed in one story yet. Pick your language in the app's settings during setup.

## Step 4: read and give feedback

Open the app each morning. Like or dislike stories so it learns. You can also ask it to avoid topics (for example work or money).

## Privacy controls

- Disconnect any app from your profile.
- Review and delete your feedback history.
- Delete everything: profile, then "Delete my data".
- Your Dreambeans choices don't change your settings in Gemini Apps or AI Mode.

## Good to know

- It is an experiment and may change or shut down.
- US downloads fell 84% in October (Sensor Tower estimate via TechCrunch), so treat it as a fun extra, not a daily planner.
- Stories are AI-written and AI-illustrated. Check dates and tickets in the original app (Calendar, Gmail) before acting.
