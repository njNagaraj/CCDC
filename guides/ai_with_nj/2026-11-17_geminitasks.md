# Tasks in Gemini: 20 tasks to copy (@ai_with_nj)

On 17 Nov 2026 Google made Tasks in Gemini free in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India (blog.google, "Put Gemini to work for you", Josh Woodward). Gemini can now carry out multi-step tasks using your connected Google apps, email, calendar and payment methods. It connects with more than 60 apps and asks for confirmation before critical steps like clicking "Buy".

## Turn it on

1. Update the Gemini app (Play Store or App Store).
2. Open Gemini and flip the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task.
3. Describe the task in plain words, in one message.
4. Check the connected apps it asks for, and confirm each critical step.

Texting Gemini and forwarding emails to gemini@google.com start in the U.S. only (texting needs a U.S. number). Google says a set of advanced features also starts in the U.S. If a task below doesn't work in India yet, that's why.

## 20 tasks to copy

Bills and money
1. "My electricity bill is much higher this month. Check my past bills in Gmail and tell me why."
2. "List every subscription I pay for, from my emails, with the monthly amount."
3. "Find my last 3 phone bills in Gmail and tell me if I'm on the cheapest plan for my usage."
4. "When are my credit card and loan due dates this month? Add reminders 3 days before."

Gmail and calendar
5. "Find the school holiday and exam dates in my emails and add them all to my calendar."
6. "Find all meeting invites I haven't answered this week and list them."
7. "Summarise unread emails from the last 2 days. Which ones need a reply today?"
8. "Draft polite replies to the 3 most urgent emails. Don't send them."
9. "Find my flight and hotel bookings in Gmail and make a trip plan in my calendar."

Shopping
10. "Compare 3 fast USB-C chargers for my phone and look for a discount code."
11. "Find a 1.5 ton 5-star inverter AC under ₹40,000 and compare the top 3."
12. "I need a birthday gift under ₹2,000 for a 10-year-old who likes science. Give me 5 options."

Students
13. "Make a 2-week study plan for my exam on [date] and put it in my calendar."
14. "Find all assignment deadlines in my college emails and add them to my calendar."
15. "Turn my notes in Google Docs into 20 quiz questions."

Office
16. "Make a to-do list from today's emails, sorted by deadline."
17. "Create a Google Doc with the agenda for tomorrow's 11 AM meeting, using the email thread."
18. "Find the last invoice from [company] in Gmail and tell me the amount and due date."

Home
19. "Plan a weekly vegetarian menu for 4 people and make a grocery list."
20. "Find a plumber near me with good reviews and list their numbers." (calling is not confirmed for India yet)

## Safe settings

- Leave payments on "ask every time". Use "Always allow" only for harmless steps.
- Connect only the apps a task needs. You can disconnect apps any time.
- Delete chat history you don't want kept.
- Tap thumbs-down on a wrong answer to report it.
