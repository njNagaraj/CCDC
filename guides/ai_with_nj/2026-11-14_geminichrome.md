# Gemini in Chrome: 15 prompts to copy (@ai_with_nj)

Chrome on your laptop has Google's Gemini built in. Google (blog.google, 12 Nov 2026): "Click the Gemini icon in your Chrome toolbar to open the side panel," then ask it to summarize a dense report, compare product details, or explain complicated topics in plain language, without leaving the page. It rolled out to India in March 2026 for personal Google accounts on Windows, macOS and ChromeOS, with 50+ more languages (Google Workspace blog).

## Turn it on

1. Update Chrome (Menu, Help, About Google Chrome).
2. Sign in to Chrome with your Google account.
3. Click the Gemini icon at the top of Chrome. If you don't see it, your account or region may not have it yet; Google's help page for Gemini in Chrome lists the requirements.

## 15 prompts

Long pages and PDFs
1. "Summarize this in 5 bullet points. What should I do next?"
2. "What are the key dates and deadlines on this page?"
3. "Make a checklist from this page."

Government, bank and forms
4. "Explain this in simple Hindi. What documents do I need?"
5. "Who is eligible for this scheme? Answer yes or no for: age 30, salaried, Tamil Nadu."
6. "List every fee or charge mentioned on this page."

Shopping
7. "Summarize the reviews. What do people complain about most?"
8. "Compare this product with the one in my other tab on price, warranty and battery." (open both pages first)
9. "Is this a good deal? What should I check before buying?"

Work
10. "Turn this article into 3 points I can share with my team."
11. "Draft a polite reply to this email in under 80 words."
12. "Explain this technical page to a non-technical manager."

Study
13. "Explain this topic simply and give me 3 practice questions."
14. "Make 10 flashcards from this page."
15. "What are the 3 most important formulas on this page and when do I use each?"

## Safety

- Gemini works with the page you share. Close the side panel on banking, medical or other private pages.
- Check every number (fees, dates, prices) on the page itself.
- On your phone, use the Gemini app instead.
